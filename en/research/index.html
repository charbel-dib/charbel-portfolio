RESEARCH EXPERIENCE

Two internships, a shared approach.

01 / Astek Innovation Lab · 2026 · March–September

Understand where a fact-checking pipeline loses quality.

Read the case study ↗
Question
How can retrieval, evidence-extraction and classification errors be distinguished within a RAG pipeline?
My contribution
Configuration comparisons, shared evaluation framework, extraction prompts, error analysis and experiment organisation.
Method
Compare chunking, hybrid retrieval and reranking configurations; evaluate the stages separately, then examine their effect on final classification.
Outcome
A diagnostic approach to locate quality losses and guide improvements using results, shared protocols and reproducible experiments.
Limits
Contributions are presented at a high level. The internal report and detailed results are not published on this website.

02 / CESI / LINEACT · 2025 · May–August

Predict relative density from process parameters.

Read the case study ↗
Question
How can manufacturing parameters help study the relative density of parts produced by laser powder bed fusion?
My contribution
Experimental database construction, model comparison and configuration analysis during my CESI / LINEACT internship. First-author contribution to the paper with Rima Hleiss.
Method
Build and structure an experimental database, analyse manufacturing configurations, use clustering and compare predictive models.
Outcome
A study connecting data, machine learning and part quality, developed into a first-author IEEE conference paper with Rima Hleiss.
Limits
The findings concern the study setting; they do not establish validation in industrial production.
