FleetGuard.

A missed failure costs more than a false alarm.

Classify APS failures in Scania trucks, choose a threshold based on error costs, and make the decision inspectable in an application.

01

The problem

On an imbalanced dataset, high accuracy can hide the most costly failures. The Scania challenge assigns a cost of 10 to a false alarm and 500 to a missed APS failure. That trade-off should drive the decision.

02

Technical decisions

01

Evaluate before optimising

Explicit baselines, duplicate-aware group splits, and separate fitting, calibration and threshold-selection roles.

02

Freeze the entire decision

Model, preprocessing, calibration and threshold are recorded together. The official test is evaluated after that freeze.

03

Make the model usable

FastAPI service, CSV batch review and a React interface. Scores and missing values remain visible to help interpret decisions.

03

What the project delivers

On the 16,000-row official test: 95.73% recall, 43.05% precision, 16 missed APS failures and 475 false alarms. Total cost: 12,750 challenge units. These results are recorded in the repository’s model card.

Scope & limits

Classification on an anonymised public dataset, not a forecast of future failures. Results do not validate use on a real fleet. AWS infrastructure is prepared in the repository; the inspected report does not establish an actual AWS deployment.
