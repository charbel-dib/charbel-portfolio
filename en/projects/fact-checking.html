Astek Innovation Lab · R&D internship · 2026

Climate fact-checking.

Evaluate a fact-checking pipeline, stage by stage.

During my final-year internship at Astek, I compared chunking, hybrid retrieval and reranking strategies, then studied evidence extraction and climate-claim classification. The aim was to identify quality losses and error propagation across the RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) pipeline.

Context
Final-year internship · March–September 2026
My contribution
Configuration comparisons, shared evaluation framework, extraction prompts, error analysis and experiment organisation.

01

What the project delivers

A diagnostic and experimental comparison method, from retrieval to the final decision. I evaluated the Phi-3 classifier, already LoRA-adapted in earlier work; I did not fine-tune it.

02

The problem

Finding the right document is not enough: the system must retrieve the right passage, extract relevant evidence and classify the claim without amplifying upstream errors.

03

Technical decisions

01

Compare retrieval units

Benchmark chunking strategies, DPR + BM25 hybrid retrieval and rerankers.

02

Separate pipeline errors

Evaluate retrieval, extraction and classification to identify where quality deteriorates.

03

Make experiments reproducible

Shared protocols, retrieval and classification metrics, GPU experiments with Docker, caching and logs.

04

Evaluation

NDCG measures passage-ranking quality, recall measures coverage, and macro-F1 balances classification performance across classes. Separating these measures helps explain why better rankings do not guarantee a better final decision.

What I take from it

Identify what actually improves the final answer, beyond an isolated component score.
