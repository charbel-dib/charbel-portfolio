ENSTA assistant

A bilingual document assistant, from ingestion to sourced answers.

A bilingual assistant indexing campus information, answering with citations and providing escalation when it cannot answer adequately. The prototype uses a RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) pipeline.

Context
Prototype, Web, PDF & XLSX corpus
My contribution
Document pipeline, answer service, session management and web integration.

Results

On 60 annotated questions in French and English, the right source is among the 8 passages sent to the model in 55 cases (91.7%). A reranker lifts the right source into the top 3 from 81.2% to 89.6% of cases. A threshold recalibrated to 0.84 turns away 10 of 11 unseen off-topic questions while keeping 10 of 12 valid ones. End-to-end prototype: FastAPI service, sessions, chat interface, embeddable widget, refusal and email escalation.

The problem

Campus information is spread across webpages, PDFs and spreadsheets. It needs to become searchable while retaining source references and handling updates.

Technical decisions

Separate ingestion from serving

Collect, clean, chunk and index documents upstream of the answering service.

Keep sources in the answer

Multilingual-E5 embeddings, a FAISS index, retrieval threshold and citations connect answers to documents.

Account for corpus updates

Daily web-corpus rebuild and index reloading without restarting the service. PDF and XLSX updates are manual, according to the academic report.

Evaluation

A 60-question main set and a 23-question held-out set of unseen questions, drawn from the indexed corpus and reviewed one by one, with expected source and facts; Hit@k and MRR, with and without reranking. With so few questions, a gap of a few points is not significant. Citations, a similarity threshold, refusal and human escalation are controls, not a guarantee against hallucinations. Final-year project supervised by Dr Abdelmalek Toumi.

What I take from it

Turn a document collection into a usable service whose answers remain connected to their origin.

Limits

Academic prototype, not officially deployed by the school. The first question set turned out optimistic: on unseen questions, the right source reaches the context in 83% of cases, versus 94%. The 0.84 threshold was chosen after seeing both sets. Answer quality itself is still to be measured.
