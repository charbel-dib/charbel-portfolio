The problem

Campus information is spread across webpages, PDFs and spreadsheets. It needs to become searchable while retaining source references and handling updates.

02

Technical decisions

01

Separate ingestion from serving

Collect, clean, chunk and index documents upstream of the answering service.

02

Keep sources in the answer

Multilingual-E5 embeddings, a FAISS index, retrieval threshold and citations connect answers to documents.

03

Account for corpus updates

Daily web-corpus rebuild and index reloading without restarting the service.

03

What the project delivers

An end-to-end prototype with a FastAPI service, sessions, chat interface and embeddable widget. Refusal and email escalation complement source-backed answers.

What I take from it

Turn a document collection into a usable service whose answers remain connected to their origin.

Scope & limits

Academic prototype: the repository does not establish an official institutional deployment or large-scale measured answer quality.
