CESI / LINEACT · Research · 2025–26

Quality in additive manufacturing.

Study relative density in laser powder bed fusion parts.

Using an experimental database linking process parameters with part properties, compare predictive models and clustering to study relative density in laser powder bed fusion (L-PBF).

Context
Internship · May–August 2025 · Conference paper · 2026
My contribution
Experimental database construction, model comparison and configuration analysis during my CESI / LINEACT internship. First-author contribution to the paper with Rima Hleiss.

The problem

L-PBF process parameters affect part relative density and porosity. The goal is to study those relationships using experimental data and predictive models.

Evaluation

The approach links structured experimental observations, model comparison and clustering of configurations to relative density, a property related to porosity and part quality. The conference paper is the public outcome of this study.
